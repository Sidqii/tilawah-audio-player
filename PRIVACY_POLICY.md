# Privacy Policy for Tilawah

Last updated: June 2026

Tilawah is a Quran audio player application that allows users to browse Surahs and listen to Quran recitations.

## Information Collection and Use

Tilawah does not collect, store, process, or share any personal information from users.

The application does not require user registration and does not request personal data such as:

* Name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Location data
* Payment information

## Third-Party Services

Tilawah may retrieve Quran content and audio streams from publicly available APIs and content providers. These services are used only to deliver application content.

## Data Sharing

Tilawah does not sell, share, or disclose user information to third parties.

## Children's Privacy

Tilawah is suitable for users of all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

## Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
